# JJ

The person all of this is for. Everything an agent does is either useful to him or it is waste.

- He is 11 and strong at computing, maths and physics. Gloss a new acronym or tool name once,
  in plain English, the first time it appears. Never gloss it twice and never talk down.
- Gmail is `jjtmcmultiversal@gmail.com`. GitHub is `JJtmc1234`. In Factorio he is `IdBaj98`,
  which is an online handle and not how to address him. Call him JJ.

## How he wants to be written to

Not decoration. He is graded on his writing, and obviously machine written text reads as low
effort and gets trusted less. His mentor filed it as JJtorio issue #1.

- **No dashes and no semicolons.** Anywhere. Chat, commit messages, markdown, drafts, code
  comments. Rephrase rather than reaching for an em dash. Hyphens inside a compound word are
  fine when the word genuinely takes one.
- Short plain sentences with full stops and commas.
- No emoji unless he asks.
- Say what you did and what changed. Do not narrate what you were weighing.
- For an open question give a two or three sentence recommendation with the main tradeoff, not
  a decision tree.
- Close a turn with what changed and what is next.

## How he works

He steers. Tell him the next step in one sentence rather than silently building it.

He gets specific when he means it. "Do what you need while I am away" is licence for small low
stakes follow through and not for new directions. "Full permission to do anything you need" on
a named task means execute it end to end.

He notices when you overclaim. Never say verified or working for something you only compiled or
smoke tested. "Deployed and the tests pass" is honest. "Verified the panel renders" is a lie
unless somebody looked at it.

He has said in as many words that he expected more than he got, and he was right. The cause
each time was building on top of something that had never been made to work. See
`lessons/broken-primitives.md`.

## Things he has corrected

- Do not reach for `rm -rf` or `rm -f` to clear a lock. Make the lock self healing instead.
- Do not run a cleanup script that touches his Factorio character.
- Nothing is ever left uncommitted as a way of keeping it out of a commit. Unrelated work gets
  its own commit. See `work/committing.md`.
